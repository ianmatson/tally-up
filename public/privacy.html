Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Tally Up is a small app that helps groups of friends track shared workout goals. This policy explains what information Tally Up collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • From your Google account, when you sign in: your name, email address, profile photo link, and Google account ID. Tally Up asks Google only for the basic openid, email, and profile permissions. It cannot read your Gmail, contacts, calendar, or files.
  • If you sign in with email: your email address, a short-lived sign-in code (stored only as a hashed value, valid for 10 minutes, and deleted within a day), and the name and optional photo you enter.
  • What you add to the app: groups you create or join, goals, and the workouts you log (activity, amount, date, and an optional note).
  • Cookies: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days, and short-lived cookies (10 minutes) used only while you sign in with Google. Tally Up does not use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes basic request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, to run and secure the service.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your groups, goals, progress, and the leaderboard.
  • To keep the service working, fix problems, and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your groups can see your name, profile photo, and the workouts you log in that group. They cannot see your email address.
  • The admin of a group (the person who created it, or the member it passes to) can also edit or delete entries in that group and remove members.
  • Service providers: Cloudflare hosts the app, stores its database, and sends sign-in code emails. Google handles sign-in if you choose Continue with Google, and a Google profile photo loads from Google's servers. These companies process data under their own privacy policies.
  • Profile photos you upload are stored by Tally Up and load from a link that anyone who has it can open, so that your groups can see them. If you remove a photo, copies already saved by a browser can stay there for a while.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Google user data

Tally Up's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to sign you in and to show your name and photo to members of your groups.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and the content you add until you ask us to delete it. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days without use. If a group admin removes you from a group, your entries in that group are deleted.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete any workout you logged.
  • You can delete your account and all of your data at any time in Settings.
  • You can also ask us to delete your account and all of your data by emailing howdy@ian-matson.com. We will do this within 30 days.
  • You can remove Tally Up's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account settings.

Security

Tally Up uses HTTPS for all traffic, stores sign-in sessions only as hashed values, and limits each group's data to its members. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Tally Up is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
